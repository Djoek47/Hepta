## Seven threads, one listener, one thread on air

A heptagon whose sides are lines of thought. They differ in length and weight because threads do. One side is lit in On-Air red: the thread being heard now. The centre dot is the listener, and it never moves.

## Four rules

**Red means live**

On-Air red marks only what is happening now. Never decoration, never a background for long text.

**The listener stays still**

When animating, threads may shift and light up; the centre dot never moves.

**Quote, don't judge**

Insights show who said what and when, with the exact words. The brand voice reports; it does not accuse.

**Family, not twins**

Ink, paper and type are shared with Entoli. Entoli's colour is voice lime; Hepta's is On-Air red. Don't mix them in one mark.
